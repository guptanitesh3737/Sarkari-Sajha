# SarkariSajha - Product Requirements & Overview

## Mission & Purpose
SarkariSajha is Nepal's premier digital career guidance platform for public sector, banking, and government career aspirants. It provides verified vacancy alerts, AI-driven job matching, Lok Sewa timed mock examinations with instant feedback, application deadline tracking, and Google Sheets synchronization.

## Core Features
1. **Verified Government Vacancy Directory**
   - Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog), Teacher Service (Sikshak Sewa), Provincial PSCs, Security, and Banking notices.
   - Filtering by organization, level, province, and application deadline.
   - Official source attribution and verified notice badges.

2. **AI Job Matcher & Resume Parser**
   - Automated CV extraction and profile analysis.
   - Skill and qualification matching against active public sector openings.
   - Recharts interactive salary distribution visualizations in NPR scale.

3. **Lok Sewa & Banking Timed Quiz Practice Engine**
   - Exam-mode timed tests with real-time scoring.
   - In-depth answers with legislative reference explanations.
   - Category-wise analytics and global candidate leaderboards.

4. **Application Pipeline Tracker & Google Sheets Sync**
   - Personal tracking pipeline (Bookmarked -> Form Drafted -> Fee Paid -> Submitted).
   - One-click export and bi-directional synchronization with Google Sheets API via Google Workspace OAuth.

5. **Admin Management Portal**
   - Moderated vacancy creation, AI document extraction review, and candidate analytics.

## Non-Official Government Disclaimer
SarkariSajha is an independent educational and career portal. It is **not** an official government entity. All AI-extracted vacancy information requires verified source checks and admin review before publishing.
